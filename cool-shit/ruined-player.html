ruined player

a minimal audio player for macOS

as a producer, previewing an audio file without a waveform was the absolute bane of my existence. macos’s alternative was their music app, which was too heavy of a software to simply play tunes.

i missed resonic when i was on windows, so i made this. it’s not as intense, and moreso designed as an extension of finder.

  • keep seek position: switch files and it carries on from the same spot. perfect for flipping between versions of a track.
  • double-click a file in finder and it just plays. everything else in that folder lines up underneath.
  • a waveform up top. click anywhere on it to jump there.
  • bit depth / sample rate, length and file size for every file, at a glance.
  • loop whatever you’re on.
  • fold the list away when all you want is the waveform.
  • make it your default player and forget about it.
