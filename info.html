how to send files for mixing

48 kHz preferred · 44.1 min 32-bit wav preferred · 16 min one folder, one open link
  1. export stems as individually as possible, not grouped or bused (unless the group has significant creative effects). send harmonies, doubles and ad-libs as their own stems, not merged with the lead.
  2. for lead vocals, include an extra dry, uncompressed stem (no reverb, delay or compression). tuning and manual edits can stay.
  3. double-check that all stems sum to the current version of your master.
  4. include a wav of your current master/mix for reference, even a rough one.
  5. make sure your download link doesn't require access permission, and send multiple files in one folder/link.
production / ableton live projects: keep third-party plugins off grouped or bussed tracks, and freeze any track that uses third-party plugins.
